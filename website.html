📖 Our Journey 📖

On February 10th 2024, we saw each other for the first time. During the AFC final, fully expecting us to never cross paths again. Then in sometime during March, we met again. We traded Insta's and there I thought again, we would never cross paths again. She would be a mutual to me, we might see each other in school once in a while etc. But then I realised I was wrong. We started talking, day after day, week after week and month after month where talking to you didn't seem like a luxury, it was a necessity. Every day, texting you and talking to you was the best thing that could happen to me all day. It went from texting, to hours long voice calls on discord, to hangouts outside at the park to eventually watching Formula 1 together in Doha. A dream of mine that I never thought would come true. We have had our fair share of moments together. Moments where we both were the happiest people on the planet, and times when we went so long without talking to each other it drove me crazy. This website was not just for me to show off my amazing programming skills, its to show you how much you mean to me and how much you have done for me. You have helped, guided, supported and motivated me when times were difficult, when no one was there to help me and when my own friends backstabbed me. You put the smile on my face on days when I didn't feel like smiling, and you pushed me to achieve so many things in my time. Sure you can argue it's down to my hard work, but the support, the patience of handling me at times, and always creating a safe space for me to talk when I was feeling or felt low. I forever thank you for everything you have done and I feel like I need to repay them in many different ways. Words can't express how grateful I am for you, the love I have for you and the way I admire everything about you. Thank you. Thank you for everything you have given me. I love you more than you would ever know 💖
